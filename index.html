Bengaluru Native Urban Forestry Care Guide

Neem (Azadirachta indica)

Best Zone: Avenues, campuses, residential parks.

Care: Fast-growing and drought-tolerant; water saplings every 2–3 days in dry months.

Peepal (Ficus religiosa)

Best Zone: Open parks, temple grounds, wide green belts.

Care: Needs space for wide roots; supports urban birdlife and dense shade canopy.

Honge / Pongamia (Millettia pinnata)

Best Zone: Roadsides, avenues, semi-dry urban soil.

Care: Hardy native nitrogen-fixer; low watering once established past year 1.

Arjuna (Terminalia arjuna)

Best Zone: Lake bunds, wetlands, moist park edges.

Care: Thrives near water bodies; stabilizes soil embankments and prevents erosion.

Indian Tulip (Thespesia populnea)

Best Zone: Urban parks, walkways, compact green corridors.

Care: Tolerates urban heat well; regular mulching improves sapling moisture retention.

Flame of the Forest (Butea monosperma)

Best Zone: Open sunny grounds, roadside medians.

Care: Full sunlight required; drought-hardy with seasonal orange-red blooms.
